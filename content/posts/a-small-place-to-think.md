This is a place to think out loud about building software and making knowledge useful.

I’m interested in the decisions behind the code: where responsibilities belong, what a test actually proves, and how a system behaves when our assumptions stop holding.

## What belongs here

Notes on .NET and TypeScript. Questions about distributed systems. Experiments with knowledge engineering and AI-assisted development.

Some posts will be practical. Others will be unfinished thoughts that become clearer through writing.

The aim is simple: leave behind something useful, and keep learning.
